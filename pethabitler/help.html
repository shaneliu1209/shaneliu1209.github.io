Help & Support

Contact shaneliu.app@gmail.com or use Send Feedback in Settings. To report a problem, include your app version and build, iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to it. Do not send your password or payment details.

Account and email

Sign up with your preferred name, email and password. Enter the confirmation code from your email in PetHabitler, then log in. You can read that email on another device. Check your spam folder if the email is missing, and request a new code if it has expired. Forgot Password on the login screen sends a recovery code; enter it in the app to choose a new password. When signed in, use Change Password in Settings.

Water goals and reminders

You can enter weight manually or choose to import it once from Apple Health. Changing weight updates the estimated goal for the current unsettled day. The stored goal uses the same rounded amount shown in the app. This estimate includes water from drinks and food; it is not a target for plain water alone. Wake-up and bedtime settings define the reminder period; changing them adjusts reminders that have not yet been sent. Allow notifications in iPhone Settings if you want reminders outside the app. Focus and Sleep settings may silence or delay delivery even when app notifications are enabled.

Daily plant growth settles at local midnight. Existing records keep their saved dates when upgrading or changing time zones. If the app is closed at that time, reopening it processes the days that have passed. Your saved drinking records are used; the app does not assume that missing records were completed.

Work, movement and eye rest

A standard work cycle leads to a five-minute movement break after 50 minutes, followed by five minutes of eye rest. A planned session shorter than 50 minutes needs no break and ends at its planned time. If you miss a break that had not started while away, return and start the next work cycle when ready.

During a running rest, tap Interrupt before leaving the app or locking the phone. You must restart and complete the whole break within its 30-minute interruption window. Leaving without Interrupt records that running break as missed. Stop a movement break if you feel discomfort. An adventure already in progress is independent of ending work; its result is processed when it is due, including when you return after being away.

Plus, restoration and cancellation

Choose a plan on the Plus screen. Prices and trial eligibility are supplied by Apple. If an existing subscription is not recognised, check that you are using the appropriate Apple Account and PetHabitler account, then use Restore Purchases. Contact support if access is still missing.

If Plus expires during a work cycle, you can finish that cycle and its corresponding rests. An active rest can also finish. New Plus work cycles wait for valid access; a failed network check does not delete progress. After renewing or restoring an active purchase, Plus features become available again.

To cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PetHabitler, or use Manage Subscription in the app. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel renewal. For refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem.

Restoring progress and deleting data

Log into the same PetHabitler account to restore an available cloud backup. Restoration needs a network connection, and unsynced changes from another device may not yet be available. If the app reports a conflict, choose carefully before replacing progress.

To remove your cloud account and saved progress, use Delete Account and Data in Settings. Account deletion is permanent after it succeeds. Logging out or uninstalling alone does not delete cloud progress. If deletion fails, the app keeps local progress and you can retry or contact support.

Creator codes and links

Open your creator’s link and tap Copy code. After installing PetHabitler and signing in, choose Have a creator code? on the plan screen, or open Settings → PetHabitler Plus → Creator Code. Tap Paste, review the code, then tap Apply Code. You can also type the code yourself. This credits their recommendation without changing your subscription price. A creator code already saved to your account is shown there. Apply it before buying a subscription so that the purchase can be credited to your creator.

Apple Health access

Weight import is optional and reads once when requested. No readable result may mean Health access was declined or no suitable weight is available. You can still type your weight and unit manually. Manage Health permissions in iPhone Settings.

Videos or reminders not working

Restart the app and check notification permissions. If a video still cannot play or a notification causes the app to close, send support the activity, app version/build, device details and approximate time. A screen recording is helpful if you are comfortable sharing it; remove private information first.
