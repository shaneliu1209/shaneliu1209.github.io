Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

PetHabitler is operated by Shihao Liu, an independent developer. This policy explains how the iPhone app handles your information. For privacy questions, contact shaneliu.app@gmail.com.

Information you provide

  • Account details: your email address, preferred name and account identifier. Supabase processes your password for authentication. We do not ask you to send your password to our support inbox.
  • Wellness entries and settings: weight, units, daily water goals, drinking records, awake hours, work schedules, movement and eye-rest records, and reminder preferences.
  • App progress: plants and growth decisions, maturity history, badges, pet condition, adventure opportunities, trips and rewards.
  • Purchase information: subscription products, transactions, trial eligibility, renewal and expiration status, and the identifiers needed to verify Plus access. Apple handles payment details; PetHabitler does not receive your full card number.
  • Optional creator code: a creator code you enter or paste and confirm, the creator name returned by Insert Affiliate, and a random attribution identifier saved with your app account.
  • Support messages: information you choose to send by email. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Apple Health

Importing weight is optional. When you tap the Apple Health import control and grant access, the app reads your latest available weight and preferred weight unit once. It does not continuously monitor Apple Health and does not write data to Apple Health. You can enter or change your weight manually instead.

The imported weight becomes part of your PetHabitler settings and may be included in your account's cloud backup, along with manually entered weight and wellness records. We use this information to estimate a drinking goal and operate the app. We do not use Apple Health information for advertising or marketing attribution. You can manage Health access in your iPhone's Settings.

Storage and account backup

The app keeps progress on your device. When you use a signed-in account, a backup of your app settings and progress is stored in our Supabase project so it can be restored for that account. This includes wellness settings and records. Our current database region is the United States. Internet access is needed to upload or restore a backup; an unsynced local change may not be available on another device.

Logging out, deleting the app or leaving TestFlight does not delete your cloud account. Use the in-app account deletion control if you want to remove it.

Why we use information

  • To create and authenticate your account, send confirmation or password-reset emails, and respond to support requests.
  • To save your records, calculate goals and plant progress, schedule reminders, run timers and adventures, and award badges.
  • To verify, restore and manage access to subscription features.
  • To credit the creator you choose and calculate affiliate commissions for qualifying purchases and renewals.
  • To protect account access, resolve errors and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and account storage. Resend delivers account emails. Gmail receives emails sent to our support address. Apple processes App Store purchases and provides HealthKit and notification services. RevenueCat verifies subscription status using your app account identifier and purchase information. Its SDK also sends Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV) as technical request metadata. This vendor identifier is different from Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA); we do not enable advertising-identifier collection or use it to match you across unrelated apps.

These providers may process necessary technical information, such as IP addresses, request metadata and service logs, to deliver and secure their services. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where a provider operates. We share the information needed for these functions; we do not sell your personal information. The app uses the official Insert Affiliate SDK to verify creator codes that you choose to submit. RevenueCat sends purchase and renewal events, the app account identifier and the selected creator attribution to Insert Affiliate for commission reporting. When you submit a creator code, the SDK can also count that lookup and report initialization using our public company identifier. Subscription activity and creator-code usage are used for subscription and commission reporting. We disable Insert Affiliate's automatic clipboard reading and device-information matching features. We do not send wellness entries or Apple Health data to Insert Affiliate or RevenueCat. This app does not display third-party advertisements.

If you open an Insert Affiliate creator link, its hosted page can record the link click. You can explicitly copy the displayed creator code there and paste it in PetHabitler. Opening the link alone does not apply the code to your app account.

GitHub Pages hosts these information pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of this information. These pages do not contain analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data and progress while they are needed to provide your account and the app. Deleting your account through the app removes its active authentication record and saved cloud progress, and clears the corresponding local account data after deletion succeeds. Limited backups, security logs, correspondence or transaction records may be retained by us or our providers as required for security, recovery or legal obligations.

After account deletion, we request removal of the associated RevenueCat customer profile. If the service is temporarily unavailable or deletion is still processing, a server queue retries the request. The queue retains the deleted account identifier and retry status until cleanup is confirmed. A purchase record associated with another active account is protected rather than deleted with that account; contact support if you need help with remaining purchase associations. Apple and service providers may retain transaction, commission or security records under their own obligations and policies. Deleting your PetHabitler account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions separately in your Apple Account.

Your choices

You can edit your app settings, decline notifications or Health access, log out, or delete your account. You can contact us to request access to or correction of your personal information, or to ask for help exercising rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify that a request belongs to your account.

Security and children

We use access controls and service-provider safeguards to protect account data. No service can guarantee absolute security. PetHabitler is a general wellness app and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can investigate.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice when required. A future integration will need to be reviewed and reflected in this policy before it is enabled.
